03 / FIELD NOTES

A reduction transfers a failure to an assumption

It is conditional reasoning, not an unconditional guarantee.

A proof constructs an algorithm that uses a successful attacker to solve an assumed-hard problem or break a lower-level primitive. Inspect query limits, reduction loss, random-oracle or ideal-cipher assumptions and parameter sizes. RSA security is not simply a general equivalence to factoring; discrete-log, DH and lattice assumptions are distinct. IND-CPA alone does not imply integrity.

Match the model to implementation

An abstract proof may not model timing, faults, parsing, weak randomness, key compromise or rollback. State those assumptions and test the actual boundary.

04 / FIELD NOTES

Perfect secrecy and computational secrecy

A one-time pad buys a strong property with demanding key management.

A uniform independent key at least as long as the message, used only once, gives one-time-pad perfect secrecy. It does not provide integrity. Stream ciphers replace the long pad with computational pseudorandomness from a short secret; the assumptions and key/nonce rules change. Neither construction should be deployed as a homemade replacement for an authenticated protocol.
